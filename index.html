What is the Nuel Human OS?

▼

The Nuel Human OS is a disciplined daily framework designed to optimize cognitive output, physical vitality, and digital boundaries. By executing these four key pillars daily, you eliminate friction and ensure compound progress:

  • Water Intake (64+ oz): Maintains optimal hydration for peak cognitive focus and physical energy.
  • Daily Exercise: Keeps the physical engine running through bodyweight work, running, or cycling.
  • No-Ping Hour: Protects your attention span by cutting off notifications, alerts, and digital noise for 60 minutes.
  • 90-Minute Deep Work (2x): Harnesses ultra-focused blocks of uninterrupted time for high-impact creation.
